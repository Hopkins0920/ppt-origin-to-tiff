# Privacy / 数据隐私

The converter has no project telemetry or automatic upload feature. It works on local files. Origin, Office and their licensing/connected services may have independent network behavior.

Work directories contain original embedded graph data, cached images, TIFFs, slide renders, absolute paths and error messages. Reports are not automatically anonymized. They may be as sensitive as the original presentation.

For public reports, provide app version, Windows/Python/Origin/Office versions, an error summary, and a synthetic minimal reproduction. Manually redact names, institution paths, project titles, license information and unpublished figure content. Crop/sanitize screenshots. Do not upload full work folders or real source PPT files by default.

中文：程序不会自动向项目维护者上传文件，但 Origin/Office 自身的联网行为独立存在。报告和中间目录含原始图表、个人路径和未发表内容，不会自动脱敏。公开反馈只附必要的脱敏错误摘要和人工合成示例。
